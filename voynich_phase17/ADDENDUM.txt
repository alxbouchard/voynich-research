ADDITIONAL DIAGNOSTIC, AFTER THE INITIAL CRIB SCAN

The homophonic, space-free search accepted multiple candidates, including a
reverse-direction window on f104r.22-23. This prompted a diagnostic against
shuffled data. This was not part of the initial protocol and is exploratory.

Use ZL, split-comma convention, literal EVA only. Make 32 fixed-seed controls
by shuffling glyph units independently within the existing clean paragraph
segments, preserving their lengths and inventories. Re-run the same forward
and reverse homophonic constraint search. Report counts and ranges only.
Do not use this post-hoc diagnostic to assign a p-value, identify a language,
or prove that the manuscript has no meaning. It asks whether mechanically
compatible windows also occur after destroying the original order.

EXTENSION CHECK, AFTER INSPECTING THE INITIAL CANDIDATES

The proposed marginal formula is followed by an uncertain short group and
then the relatively legible Latin-alphabet word maria. For every compatible
window, test whether maria can occur after a gap of 0 through 8 glyph units
in the same paragraph segment and direction. Keep the existing mapping
fixed; previously unmapped cipher units may receive a value only if the
resulting combined mapping remains deterministic. Gap units are unconstrained.
This deliberately permissive diagnostic is post-hoc and not an independent
held-out validation. It concerns a literal copied formula only; translation,
abbreviation, alternate readings and other cipher families are not tested.
