# Phase 27 technical specification

The frozen experiment is a compatibility filter for fixed bijections at each exact word length and character position. It cannot recover keys and does not test semantic meaning.

For each packet, take exactly n=150 occurrence tokens at each L∈{4,5,6,7}. For each i=0,…,L−2, calculate the empirical conditional entropy from the joint and first-column marginal counts. Average positions within each L, then average the four length means equally. All counts, per-position entropies and aggregate values are archived. There is no smoothing or bias correction in the measurement.

If ciphertext C_i=π_(L,i)(P_i) with each π a bijection fixed over the packet population, joint contingency counts are only row/column relabelled. Their entropy, the marginal entropy, and their difference are unchanged. This includes the two-table initial/final ciphers and a larger length/position-specific family. It excludes insertions, deleted characters, homophony, many-to-one maps, wrong segmentation, variable-length character units and tables changing by occurrence. The theorem concerns exact empirical counts; inference from a small set of known plaintext corpora to an unknown historical plaintext is a separate, limited empirical assumption.

Reference packets are disjoint within each archived reference source. The interval [minimum(T)−0.20, maximum(T)+0.20] is defined by twelve packets per source and a frozen rule. It is not a confidence interval. Six validation packets per language come from different books; the Italian author is shared. Mechanical checks and at least 5/6 within-band validation packets in both languages are required before the manuscript is measured. All 36 encrypted variants must preserve T and H_position to 1e−12. No key or plaintext is supplied to `measure.py`.

Manuscript parsing reads the copied raw IVTFF source, joins physical continuation lines, applies inline metadata, rejects phantom and non-P0/P1 loci, selects literal tokens and known A/B/section/hand metadata, and excludes f85/f86 and unassigned composites. It does not merge uncertain spaces or choose alternatives. Raw source line numbers and token occurrence IDs are retained. Whole physical folios are partitioned by floor(folio_number/5) mod 2. Two nonoverlapping packets per group and fold are selected without replacement within length. ZL is primary; IT is transcription sensitivity, not a second manuscript. These are exposed-source internal partitions. The exact availability and selected folio/section/hand composition are archived.

For each group/reference pair, the prespecified criterion requires all four packets to be outside the band on the same side. The result is an operational incompatibility with these references and assumptions, not a formal universal language rejection. No p-values, nominal error rates, parameter tuning or additional tokenization sensitivity are introduced after measurement.

`verify.py` was added after results as an arithmetic/provenance audit. It computes conditional entropy directly as the count-weighted sum of log2(n_x/n_xy), rather than reusing the primary entropy difference. It independently reconstructs every encrypted plaintext, checks the suffix removal, verifies the preserved column histograms, reads all sampled manuscript tokens from raw source lines, checks nonoverlap and whole-folio allocation, and rebuilds band/classification decisions. `replay.py` reproduces the frozen source-to-result pipeline in an isolated directory and checks exact bytes. These are same-operator software checks, never independent historical or linguistic validation.

Sampling seeds, generators, prior exposure, source hashes and stopping rules are specified in `PROTOCOL.json` and `FREEZE.json`. Current outputs retain 24 references, 110 controls and 16 manuscript packets. Derived cipher versions, word-shuffled controls and source-overlapping suffix controls must not be counted as independent authors or independent messages. All unknowns and negative/non-specific outcomes remain included. `NOT_TRANSLATION`.
